gitsong · a year of commits, out loud

@madhavbuilds's year,
played as a song.

Every week of the contribution graph is one beat. Every day in it is one note. Busy days hit harder, busy weeks bring in the drums, and the empty weeks go quiet.

@madhavbuilds
 press play
Week = beatSunday to Saturday plays as a fast arpeggio inside each beat, low to high.
Day = noteEach day is a tone of the current chord. Shade sets how hard it hits.
Busy = loudBusy weeks add bass, kick and hats. Your best days shimmer an octave up.
Quiet = quietWeeks off leave only the pad. You can hear every break you took.
